## 🚀 Step-by-Step Rollout Checklist

### Phase 1: Deploy the Landing Page & Download Link (Day 1 — ~15 mins)

1. **Host the Website**:
   - Go to [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com) (or [Vercel](https://vercel.com)).
   - Upload the `landing` directory: `C:\Users\subti\repos\scribe-dictation\landing`
   - Point your custom domain (e.g. `privacyscribe.app`) to it.
2. **Host the `.exe` Download**:
   - Upload `dist/PrivacyScribe.exe` to a GitHub Release, Google Drive link, or Cloudflare R2 bucket.
   - Update the "Download Free" button link inside `landing/index.html` (if necessary).
