## 1. Hacker News (Show HN)

**Target URL**: `https://news.ycombinator.com/submit`
**Best Timing**: Tuesday or Wednesday morning (8:00 AM – 10:00 AM EST).

### Title:
> Show HN: Privacy Scribe – 100% offline Whisper AI voice dictation for desktop (no subscription)

### Body / First Comment:
```text
Hey HN,

I got tired of modern voice dictation tools requiring $15/month subscriptions and streaming raw microphone audio to third-party cloud servers. For legal documents, private codebases, medical notes, or personal journaling, cloud dictation is often a non-starter.

I built Privacy Scribe (https://privacyscribe.app) — a lightweight, 100% local desktop dictation assistant for Windows and macOS.

How it works:
1. Hold your hotkey (e.g. F8, Ctrl+Win, or Caps Lock) anywhere in your OS.
2. Speak naturally — a lightweight floating frosted-acrylic audio visualizer indicates live capture.
3. Release the key — local Whisper models (powered by CTranslate2 / faster-whisper) transcribe the speech offline, restore focus to your active window, and auto-paste directly at your cursor.

Tech details & features:
- Core engine: Faster-Whisper + CTranslate2 with CPU/GPU int8/float16 quantization for low-latency inference.
- Focus Restoration: Uses native Win32/macOS thread-input attachment & foreground bypass to ensure atomic paste delivery into target windows (Notion, VS Code, Slack, Obsidian, Word, etc.).
- Text Transformation: Built-in quick palette (Ctrl+Alt+T) to reformat transcriptions into bullet points, executive summaries, or clean prose.
- Pricing: Free tier available, $29 one-time lifetime license for unlimited dictations (no recurring subscriptions).

I'd love feedback on latency, model accuracy across accents, and overall desktop workflow feel!
```
